Asset Transfer SOPs

Standard Operating Procedures for transferring 14 categories of digital and physical assets. Built from Empire Flippers' vetted-marketplace process, adapted for the VibeFlippers + FlipWala ecosystem.

⚠️ Important — how this works: VibeFlippers and FlipWala provide these SOPs as guidance. The platform is not a party to any transaction between buyer and seller. Parties execute their own transfers per the steps below; brokers may guide and help renegotiate, but are not responsible for execution. Use a qualified attorney for legal review on deals over $10K. Use Escrow.com (linked in deal rooms) for funds custody on deals over $1K.
