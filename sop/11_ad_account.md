# Ad Account (Meta / Google / TikTok / X)

## Asset Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Asset type | Aged ad account with positive history (no ToS strikes, no permanent restrictions, established spend history) |
| Primary platform | **VibeFlippers** |
| Cross-listable to FW? | No |
| Typical price range | $200–$10K (rare to exceed) |
| Typical valuation | Account age + verified spend × multiplier (varies by platform restriction risk) |
| EF equivalent | NOT in EF catalog |
| Min listing threshold | Account age ≥6 months; positive spend history ≥$10K cumulative |
| Operator interview required? | Always (high fraud risk) |

> ⚠️ **Platform ToS risk:** All major ad platforms prohibit account transfers/sales. This is a **gray-market asset** — disclose risk in APA, sale by Business Manager admin role transfer rather than account ownership when possible.

## What's typically included

- Ad account access (via Business Manager role transfer)
- Audience library (custom audiences, lookalikes, retargeting pools)
- Pixel/tag history
- Creative library
- Campaign history + performance data
- (Sometimes) connected payment method removed; buyer adds own

## What's typically excluded

- Personal Meta/Google/TikTok account (the human)
- Outstanding ad balances (must be settled)
- Pixels tied to seller's domain (buyer re-installs on their domain)

## Ad-account-specific gotchas

| Gotcha | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Meta/Google/TikTok ToS prohibit account sales** | Use BM admin role transfer (not account ownership) where possible; APA discloses risk |
| **Account locked after credential changes** | Use BM admin transfer (no credential changes) |
| **Permanent restriction post-purchase** | APA refund clause if banned within 14 days for pre-existing reason |
| **Pixel data tied to seller's domain** | Buyer re-installs on own domain; data stays with account |
| **Restricted verticals (crypto, gambling, supplements)** | Disclose; buyer accepts in writing |
| **Payment method liability** | Seller's method removed; buyer's method added before any spend |
| **Cross-account links (page, Instagram)** | Re-link to buyer's assets post-transfer |
| **Geographic ad targeting limitations** | Account region may not match buyer's target |
| **Spending limit unlocked vs. capped** | Verify daily/lifetime spend limits |
| **Two-step verification on account** | Reset to buyer's authenticator |

## Operator checks
- Account standing (BM dashboard)
- Spend history consistency
- Recent creative compliance review
- Verify no automated rules with seller's external dependencies
- Verify no scheduled campaigns about to spend during transfer
